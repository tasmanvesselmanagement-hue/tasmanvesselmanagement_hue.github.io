The problem

The plan is only as good as the binder it's stuck in

MTOPs are written to satisfy an audit, then filed. In practice, most of what keeps a vessel compliant — checklists, drills, maintenance sign-offs, certificate renewals — still happens on paper, in someone's head, or in a spreadsheet nobody else opens.

Before

The binder on the shelf

  • MTOP referenced twice a year, at audit time
  • Checklists photocopied, filled in by hand, filed loose
  • Certificate expiries tracked from memory
  • Maintenance records split across logbooks and texts
  • One person holds the whole picture
With TVM

The plan, live on every device

  • MTOP procedures become guided digital checklists
  • Crew complete sign-offs on deck, on a phone, offline
  • Certificate and survey dates flag themselves early
  • One asset and maintenance register for the whole fleet
  • Anyone authorised can see exactly where things stand
The platform

Customised to your operation, not the other way round

Most compliance software asks you to adapt your procedures to fit its forms. This is built the other way — starting from your MTOP, your vessels, and how your crew actually works.

01

Your MTOP, structured

Every procedure, checklist and drill in your operator plan becomes a digital workflow, laid out the way Maritime NZ expects to see it at audit.

02

Works on the water

Built for the reality of a vessel — patchy or no signal, wet hands, deck conditions. Entries sync when connectivity returns.

03

Nothing to relearn

The system follows your existing terms, roles and sign-off chain. Crew are guided through what they already know how to do.

04

Audit-ready by default

Every completed checklist, drill and inspection is timestamped and stored, ready to hand to a Maritime NZ auditor without a scramble.

05

Fleet-wide visibility

See every vessel's compliance status, outstanding actions and upcoming renewals from one place, not one binder per boat.

06

Grows with the operation

Started as MTOP digitisation. Now extends into full asset management — the same register, more of what it can tell you.
